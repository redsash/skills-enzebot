# CMU Modular Masonry Guide

Reference standards for designing Concrete Masonry Unit (CMU) structures without cutting blocks in the field.

---

## 1. Modular Dimensions

Standard CMU blocks in the US and Caribbean follow a nominal 8-inch module:
- **Standard Stretcher:** Nominal $8" \times 8" \times 16"$ (Actual: $7\frac{5}{8}" \times 7\frac{5}{8}" \times 15\frac{5}{8}"$ with $\frac{3}{8}"$ mortar joint).
- **Half Block:** Nominal $8" \times 8" \times 8"$ ($7\frac{5}{8}" \times 7\frac{5}{8}" \times 7\frac{5}{8}"$).

### Dimensional Rules
1. **Wall Lengths & Spans:** Every exterior span, column bay, and corner-to-corner length should be a multiple of **8 inches** (half-block) and ideally **16 inches** (full block).
2. **Wall Heights:** Finished floor-to-ceiling heights should snap to multiples of **8 inches**:
   - 8'-0" (12 courses)
   - 8'-8" (13 courses)
   - 9'-4" (14 courses)
   - 10'-0" (15 courses)
   - 10'-8" (16 courses)
3. **Rough Openings for Doors & Windows:**
   - Widths must equal modular opening dimension plus mortar allowance (multiples of 8" or 16" plus joint width).
   - Door rough opening: 3'-0" door requires 3'-4" (40") masonry opening or 3'-0" pre-hung frame with anchor clips.

## 3. Structural Reinforcement & Wind Defense

- **Vertical Cores:** Grouted cells with #4 or #5 rebar spaced at 32" or 48" on center, and at all corners, jambs, and wall intersections.
- **Bond Beams:** Continuous reinforced horizontal lintel/bond beam courses at the top of wall plates and above all window/door heads to resist hurricane wind loads.
- **Exterior Step-Downs:** Maintain a 1.5" to 2" vertical step down from interior slab to exterior lanai/terrace slabs to prevent rain infiltration during gale-force winds.
