# 5D Strategic Thinking: Theoretical & Operational Reference

This document provides foundational theory, developmental lineages, and prompt assets supporting the `strategic-thinking-5d` skill.

---

## 1. Theoretical Lineages

Dan Koe’s 5D Thinking framework synthesizes several major pillars of 20th- and 21st-century developmental and systems theory:

### A. Ken Wilber’s Integral AQAL (All Quadrants, All Lines)
- **Quadrants**: Reality is quadrupolar. Every event has:
  - An inside (subjective / intersubjective) and an outside (objective / interobjective).
  - An individual aspect (micro) and a collective aspect (macro).
- **Psychographs & Lines**: A human or an organization does not have a single developmental level. Development unfolds along multiple semi-independent lines: cognitive, moral, emotional, aesthetic, technical, kinesthetic, and interpersonal.
- **Altitude**: The overall center of gravity. Under acute stress or crisis, individuals and systems temporarily regress to their lowest stable altitude.

### B. Robert Kegan’s Constructive-Developmental Psychology
- **Subject-Object Shifts**: The engine of vertical evolution. What you are *subject to* (embedded in, cannot see, lens you look through) must become *object to* you (something you can see, evaluate, critique, and direct).
- **The Orders of Mind**:
  - *Socialized Mind (Order 3 / Level 1 Conformist)*: Identity is derived from the tribe, party, or institution.
  - *Self-Authoring Mind (Order 4 / Level 2 Individualist)*: Internal seat of judgment; generates own rules, questions dogma, but can become rigid in defending its system.
  - *Self-Transforming Mind (Order 5 / Level 3 Synthesist)*: Understands that all systems and models are partial, holding multiple paradoxes simultaneously.

### C. Clare Graves & Don Beck’s Spiral Dynamics
- **Tier 1 vs. Tier 2 Thinking**:
  - *Tier 1 (Levels 1 & 2)*: Exclusivist. Believes its worldview is the only valid way to perceive reality. Fights zero-sum cultural and ideological battles.
  - *Tier 2 (Levels 3 & 4)*: Integrational. Understands that all stages have a healthy function and purpose in human and organizational evolution. Focuses on orchestrating natural flow rather than forcing dogmatic conformity.

### D. Arthur Koestler’s Holons & Holarchies
- **Transcend and Include**: Every entity is simultaneously a whole in itself and a part of a greater whole (a *holon*).
- Cells transcend and include molecules. If you poison the molecules, the cell perishes.
- When applying 5D thinking to strategy, innovations that "transcend" legacy structures must carefully "include" the essential functions the legacy structure provided.

## 2. Portable Prompt Library

Below are ready-to-use prompts adapted for different strategic tasks.

### Prompt Template 1: POC & Architecture Stress-Test (Mode A)

```text
Context: We are designing and evaluating an architecture / product POC. I do not want superficial praise or obvious technical checklists. I need to stress-test this against hidden failure modes across multiple dimensions of reality.

Topic / POC Details:
[Paste POC description, architecture diagrams, or product thesis here]

Task: Run this POC through the 5D Strategic Thinking diagnostic:

1D (Horizontal vs. Vertical):
- Identify where we are trying to solve a structural operating-system problem with mere "horizontal" feature additions or data.
- What is the unexamined cognitive framing that limits this design?

2D (Lines, Levels & Altitude):
- Name 3 orthogonal disciplines (outside software/tech) that have solved similar problems. What is the counter-intuitive lesson from the most unexpected one?
- What adjacent, non-technical capability (governance, incentive alignment, distribution friction) is the weakest link that will bottleneck this POC?

3D (Cognitive Evolution):
- State the Level 1 consensus engineering approach (what 90% of teams would build).
- State the Level 2 contrarian critique (why typical cynics or purists say it won't work).
- Synthesize the Level 3 architecture (how to hold both truths in dynamic balance).
- Hypothesize the Level 4 generative paradigm (what category-defining shift this unlocks).

4D (The AQAL Quadrants):
- UL (Psychology): What friction, identity resistance, or user fear does this create?
- UR (Mechanics): What observable workflows, measurable steps, or code invariants are non-negotiable?
- LL (Culture): How does this align with or violate current developer/user cultural norms?
- LR (Systems): How does this navigate regulatory, economic, and infrastructure lock-ins?

5D (Evolution & Time):
- What foundational baseline must be preserved at all costs (transcend & include)?
- What does this system look like in 1 year vs 10 years as AI/agentic infrastructure matures?

Deliverable: Provide the 5-part diagnostic followed by a concrete Go/No-Go threshold and 3 immediate validation experiments for this week.
```

### Prompt Template 2: Anti-Slop Content & Technical Proposal (Mode B)

```text
Context: I want to author a high-signal article, proposal, or RFC on this topic. I refuse to publish generic AI slop, shallow platitudes, or statistical-mean filler.

Topic: [Paste topic / core argument here]
Target Audience: [Paste reader profile / stakeholders here]

Instructions:
Before writing a single paragraph of prose, execute the 5D pre-computation:
- Lines: 3 fields outside the obvious domain. Pick the most surprising one.
- Levels: State the beginner/consensus take everyone already writes. Then state the 10-year veteran take. We will write strictly from the veteran take.
- Altitude: Name the adjacent weakness blocking the reader/stakeholder on this topic.
- Quadrants: Detail the reader's private feeling (UL), actual habit (UR), cultural tribe belief (LL), and systemic pressure (LR).
- Time: The 1-year and 10-year trajectory if nothing changes.

Output the 5 findings as a concise bulleted list.
Once displayed, write the draft adhering to these criteria:
- Plain, specific, declarative sentences. No hype, no fluff, no motivational closers.
- Cut any sentence that could appear in a generic article on this topic.
- Insert `[your specific case study / metric here]` where authentic first-party proof is required.
- Weave at least 3 of the pre-computed findings directly into the narrative tension.
```
